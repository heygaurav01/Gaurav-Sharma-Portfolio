How a lead flows
01
Enquiry

A buyer submits a form on a landing page or a Meta, Google or Taboola ad.

02
Queued safely

Every enquiry enters a cloud message queue first, so no lead is lost in a spike or outage.

03
Checked & assigned

Source and UTM are recorded, duplicates flagged, and the lead goes to the next agent on that project.

04
Instant alert

The agent and managers are notified by WhatsApp, email and team chat.

05
Agent works it

Calls, remarks and status changes are logged from web or mobile. Reminders fire if follow-up slips.

06
Managers track

Dashboards and daily reports by agent, project and campaign.
